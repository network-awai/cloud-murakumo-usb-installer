DATA & RECOVERY

Keep your work with you.

Save a recovery copy of your local app data.

Export a copy

Download the Kaikei journal and Dandori board as one JSON file. The copy contains your app data in plain text.

Restore on this browser

A valid copy can be restored into an empty workspace. Existing work is preserved; restore refuses to overwrite it.

System recovery

NixOS generations provide the system rollback foundation. App recovery copies are separate from the OS generation. Distributed recovery and physical power-loss acceptance remain unqualified.
